Our Welsh Slate Serving Boards are ethically sourced from the famous quarries of North Wales, where slate has been hewn for centuries. Each board is hand-finished by skilled craftspeople to create a smooth, food-safe serving surface that only improves with use.

Slate is naturally anti-bacterial, making it ideal for food preparation and serving. It also retains temperature well — keep it in the fridge before serving cold charcuterie, or warm it gently for a rustic bread-serving experience.

Care Instructions

  • Hand wash with warm soapy water — do not put in dishwasher
  • Dry thoroughly after washing
  • Apply a light coat of food-safe mineral oil annually to maintain appearance
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to high heat
