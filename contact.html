Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book a table at the café?

Walk-ins are always welcome. However, we recommend booking for parties of 4 or more, and for afternoon tea we require at least 24 hours' notice. Call us or use the contact form above to reserve.

Is there parking at The Old Station?

Free parking is available at Raven Square Car Park, just a 2-minute walk from our entrance. There are also disabled parking bays directly adjacent to the building.

Are dogs welcome?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the outdoor seating area of the café. We always have fresh water on hand for our four-legged visitors!

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes! We offer vegetarian and vegan options on our menu, and can accommodate most dietary requirements with advance notice. Please let us know when booking or speak to a member of staff on arrival.

Can I hire the venue for a private event?

The Old Station is an atmospheric venue for private parties, corporate events, product launches and celebrations. Please get in touch via the contact form above and select 'Events & Private Hire' to start the conversation.
