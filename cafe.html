About the Café

Freshly Made, Every Day

Nestled within the original station waiting rooms of The Old Station, Platform Café combines the warmth of a traditional tea room with the quality of a modern artisan café.

Our head chef Tom Davies creates every dish from scratch using locally sourced Welsh produce where possible. From our famous Welsh Breakfast to our indulgent afternoon tea, every visit is a treat.
